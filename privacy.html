Privacy policy

Effective 19 November 2026. Applies to the "Warhammer Shorts uploader" application.

What the app does

The app is used only by the owner of the YouTube channel @flydge.warhammer to upload that channel's own videos and to read the channel's own basic information (channel id and title), through the YouTube Data API.

Data it accesses

  • OAuth access granted by the channel owner (scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly).
  • The channel id and title, used only to confirm uploads go to the right channel.

Storage and sharing

The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computer (the macOS Keychain). No data from Google accounts is sent to any third party, sold, or used for advertising. The app collects no data about viewers or other users.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
